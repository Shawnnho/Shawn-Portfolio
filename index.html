Aikido Shinju-Kai admin system

A centralised admin system for a real aikido club with several dojos. It replaces spreadsheets and manual follow-ups with one place for classes, gradings, belt promotions, payments, reports, reminders and documents.

  • Tech lead and full-stack developer, working in Scrum with a team of 5
  • Led our move from plain HTML/CSS/JS to React on Next.js
  • Weekly class schedule with instructor availability and clash checks
  • Fees by Stripe, PayNow, cash or bank transfer, with financial reports that export to PDF and Excel
  • Automated 9 AM reminder emails for term fees, grading fees and membership renewals
  • Document storage with access control and a log of every view and download
  • Pitched the project at Byte Hacks in November 2026

Beyond the classroom

Talks, workshops and community work, and what each one taught me.

21 May 2026

IBM Z industry talk

I used to think mainframes were outdated. Then I learned they still process about 90% of credit card transactions, with near-zero downtime and AI running on the platform for fraud detection.

What I took awayFinancial software can't lose data silently. This talk confirmed that back-end work, and fintech, is where I want to be.
22 July 2026

Salesforce AI workshop

About eight hours of hands-on Trailhead modules on Agentforce and Prompt Builder. I configured an AI service agent that handles bookings and recommendations, with rules that keep it within safe limits.

What I took awayA useful AI feature depends on clear logic, strict rules and clean data behind it.

Teaching seniors to use AI

Service learning at Yong-en Active Hub (YEAH!). I coached a senior one-on-one on using ChatGPT. When the slides were too small to read, I turned our deck into a QR code they could follow on their phones.

What I took awayExplaining tech clearly, with patience, and designing for people who are often left behind.
